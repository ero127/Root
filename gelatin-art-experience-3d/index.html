The craft

Turning desserts into edible art. Every jello, mousse and cheesecake is homemade and drawn by hand, so no two are ever the same.

01

All organic ingredients

Real fruit, real dairy, real flavor. Ingredients you can feel good about serving.

02

No artificial dyes

Those vivid colors come from fruit, flowers and natural ingredients, never from a bottle of dye.

03

Petals drawn inside

Flowers are drawn into crystal-clear jelly one petal at a time. Edible art, made by hand.

The process

Watch it bloom.

  1. 01

    Pour the clear

    A crystal dome sets overnight: our canvas.

  2. 02

    Draw the petals

    Ring by ring, each petal is shaped inside the jelly.

  3. 03

    Grow the flower

    Layers of color build depth, from blush to deep magenta.

  4. 04

    Fill the base

    A creamy, flavored base seals the art in. Unmold, shine, deliver.
